You’re already connected.

Small Wins automatically saves the same household plan for both phones through its Google Sheets backend. Neither phone needs a backend URL, Google account, or sign-in.

Choose your name when first opening the app. Your device remembers you; household updates appear automatically while the app is open.

Previous device-only plans remain available as backups in Household settings. They are not automatically merged into the shared plan.

The household app is open by choice. Anyone with its URL can view and edit the plan. The backing sheet itself is restricted to its owner and the account running the script.
